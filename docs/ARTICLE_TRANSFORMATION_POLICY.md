# Article Transformation Policy

## Purpose

Subject Matter transforms an existing article into a subject-focused version. It is not an encyclopedia, biography service, company-profile service, or general knowledge replacement.

## Non-negotiable transformation rules

1. Preserve the complete article. Never summarize, shorten, compress, or replace paragraphs with a general description.
2. Make the minimum edit required to remove the targeted identity bias while preserving the message, concept, facts, chronology, evidence, and legitimate disagreement.
3. Preserve factual names of people, companies, organizations, advertisers, brands, places, products, offers, contact details, historical events, and technical labels when they are necessary to the subject.
4. Preserve advertiser identity and the factual offer. Do not remove a brand, company, product, service, price, eligibility condition, location, or call to action merely because the copy is promotional. Revise only the unsupported claim around it.
5. Never replace advertiser-specific wording with a generic category description when the specific identity or offer is factual.
6. Do not remove a name merely because it is a name. A factual name is not itself identity bias.
7. Preserve labels such as Higgs boson when they are scientific or technical terms, not personal identity claims.
8. In ordinary subject transformations, revise identity-centered evaluative wording only when it makes a claim such as first, best, biggest, tallest, most, leading, famous, genius, or similar status language.
9. Do not convert a biography, company profile, historical fact report, sports transfer, or celebrity/politician article into a normal mission example. Use those only as explicit edge-case tests.
10. The original and modified article must be shown in full with a line-by-line diff. Any shortening or factual loss is a failed transformation.
11. The modified result must not analyze the author, poster, personality, behavior, or history.

## Review test

A reviewer must be able to answer: What exact identity-biased claim changed? What facts were preserved? Did the article remain complete? Did advertiser identity and the factual offer remain visible? If no specific identity-centered claim was changed, keep the original.

## Identity-bearing article categories

For articles about a person, film, company, organization, politician, advertiser, product, or other identity-bearing subject:

- Keep the identity and names when they are factual and necessary to understand the article.
- Do not remove a name merely because the subject is famous or identifiable.
- Work around adjectives, superlatives, emotional framing, marketing language, and unproved claims.
- Convert unsupported claims into neutral, attributed wording when the claim itself is relevant; do not silently delete material facts.
- Preserve evidence, dates, events, quotations, offers, terms, and competing viewpoints.
- Never shorten the article to make it appear neutral.
