# Advertising Policy

Advertising must be clearly labelled and reviewed before publication. Campaigns may target the current subject, category, language, broad region, placement, schedule, and budget. SM does not sell contributor identity, public authorship, private history, or behavioural profiles.

Advertisers must not make false or misleading claims, hide material conditions, impersonate others, promote fraud, or target prohibited goods or services. Sensitive or regulated categories require additional legal review. Campaign reports are aggregated.

Commercial emails or messages require a separate consent and unsubscribe workflow before production. The advertising team must retain approval evidence, campaign versions, complaints, and takedown decisions.
