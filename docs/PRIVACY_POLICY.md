# Subject Matter Privacy Policy

**Status:** implementation baseline; obtain Ontario/Canadian legal review before public launch.

Subject Matter collects account information needed to authenticate contributors, operate listings, process payments, prevent abuse, respond to reports, and meet legal obligations. Public listings are subject-only and do not display contributor identity or history.

We limit collection to what is reasonably necessary. Security records such as IP address, timestamps, session data, moderation records, and breach records are restricted, retained only under the published retention schedule, and are not used for person-level behavioural profiling.

Approved contributor content may remain temporarily on a contributor node. Contributor AI models must receive only the minimum data needed for posting and must follow the SM subject-only policy. Cross-border processing and service providers must be disclosed before production use.

Users may request access to or correction/deletion of their personal information through the private account workflow. Some records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, payment accounting, or law.

Questions or privacy requests: publish a real privacy contact before launch. This document is not a substitute for legal advice.
