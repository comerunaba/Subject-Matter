# Subject Matter Terms of Use

**Status:** implementation baseline; obtain legal review before public launch.

Subject Matter provides a temporary classified marketplace for subject-based listings. Visitors may browse without an account. Contributors are responsible for the accuracy, legality, and rights associated with submitted material.

Users must not post fraud, illegal goods or services, threats, harassment, hate, malware, deceptive claims, personal information about another person, or content that violates intellectual-property rights. SM may pause, remove, expire, or reject content under its policies and may preserve private security records when necessary.

Public identity is intentionally hidden, but account, payment, and security records may exist privately. SM does not guarantee continuous availability of contributor-hosted temporary content.

Paid placement is a service, not an endorsement of the listing. Prices, taxes, duration, refund eligibility, and publication status must be shown before payment. A paid listing is not published until payment and moderation requirements are satisfied.
