# Paid Placement and Refund Policy

**Status:** implementation baseline; final terms and tax treatment require legal/accounting review.

Before checkout, SM must show the plan, price, currency, applicable taxes, duration, placement, publication conditions, and refund rules. Payment confirms a request for paid placement; publication still requires moderation approval.

If SM rejects a paid listing for a policy reason, the operator must apply the disclosed refund or credit rule. Duplicate payments, failed publication caused by SM, and unauthorized transactions require investigation and correction. Refunds must be recorded against the original payment and receipt.

The current repository payment provider is a demo and must not be used to accept real money.
