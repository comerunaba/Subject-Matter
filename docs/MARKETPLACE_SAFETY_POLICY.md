# Marketplace Safety Policy

SM reviews submitted listings before publication and accepts reports from visitors. Reports may identify spam, fraud, harassment, unsafe content, misleading claims, illegal activity, privacy violations, or intellectual-property concerns.

Moderators may request changes, reject, remove, expire, or escalate a listing. A reporter's identity is not displayed publicly. Decisions must be recorded with the reason, policy reference, reviewer, and time. Contributors should have an appeal path before production launch, except where immediate removal is required for safety or law.

SM may preserve relevant private records and cooperate with valid legal requests. Public removal must not reveal the contributor's identity. Operators must maintain a documented emergency process for imminent threats, child-safety concerns, fraud, and law-enforcement requests.

## Article transformation safety

SM must distinguish identity bias from factual identity. A person's, company's, organization's, place's, product's, or historical event's name may be necessary to preserve the meaning of an article and must not be removed automatically. Safety review should target identity-centered claims and person-level analysis, not factual references.

Article transformation must preserve the complete original message and must not summarize or shorten it. A full original-versus-modified comparison is required. Company profiles, biographies, historical-fact reports, sports transfers, and celebrity or politician articles are not ordinary mission examples unless the test explicitly targets this boundary.
